How your data is handled

What MyBudget stores, how it is protected, and what to know while it is in testing. Last updated 11 November 2026.

Who runs MyBudget

MyBudget is made by Andreea Sandu of Andreea Tech. For any question about your data, use the contact form.

What is stored

  • Your email (to log in), an optional first and last name, and your chosen currency.
  • A profile photo and a description, if you choose to add them.
  • The budget data you enter: transactions, categories, wallets, budgets, savings goals, recurring items, and an activity history of what you changed.
  • Messages you send through the contact form, with the name and email you choose to add.

Demo accounts

The demo creates a temporary account filled with example data, separate from every other account. It is deleted when you log out, when you sign up for a real account, and automatically about a day after it was created.

How it is protected

  • Passwords are stored in scrambled form (hashed), so nobody can read them back.
  • The live site works over HTTPS only, uses secure cookies, and tells browsers to always use HTTPS (this is called HSTS).
  • Every account can only open its own data.
  • Too many sign-up or login attempts are blocked for a while, which slows down password guessing.

What MyBudget does not do

  • No ads and no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No third-party fonts or scripts: everything the pages need is served from this site, so your browser does not contact other companies when you visit.
  • Only two cookies: one keeps you signed in, the other stops other websites from submitting forms on your behalf.

Export or delete your data

You can export any filtered list of transactions as CSV, Excel or PDF from the Transactions page, at any time.

You can also delete your account yourself from your profile page. Your account and everything in it, including a profile photo, is removed right away.

This is a testing version

  • While MyBudget is in testing, please don't enter highly sensitive information, such as bank logins or card numbers, in notes or names.
  • Your email address isn't verified, and there is no password reset yet. If you get locked out, send a message through the contact form.
  • It is free while in testing, and features may change.
