How your data is handled

Plain language, no legal filler. Last updated 11 November 2026.

Who runs MyBudget

MyBudget is built and run by Andreea Sandu under Andreea Tech (see About). For any question about your data, use the contact form.

What is stored

  • Your email (to log in), an optional first and last name, and your chosen currency.
  • An optional profile photo and description, only if you add them.
  • The budget data you enter: transactions, categories, wallets, budgets, savings goals, recurring items, and an activity history of what you changed.
  • Messages you send through the contact form, with the name and email you choose to add.

Demo accounts

"Try the demo" creates a temporary account filled with example data, separate from every other account. It is deleted when you log out, when you create a real account, and automatically about a day after it was created. Nothing you type in the demo is kept.

How it is protected

  • Passwords are stored hashed, never in readable form.
  • In production the site forces HTTPS, uses secure cookies and sends an HSTS header.
  • Every account can only open its own data.
  • Sign-up and login attempts are rate-limited to slow down guessing.

What MyBudget does not do

  • No ads and no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No third-party fonts or scripts: everything the pages need is served from this site, so your browser does not contact other companies when you visit.
  • Only two cookies, both needed to keep you signed in and the forms safe (session and CSRF).

Your data is yours to take or delete

You can export any filtered list of transactions as CSV, Excel or PDF from the Transactions page, at any time.

You can also delete your account yourself from your profile page. Your account and everything in it, including a profile photo, is removed right away.

This is a testing version

  • Please do not enter real banking passwords or highly sensitive information while MyBudget is in testing.
  • Your email address is not verified, and there is no automatic password reset yet. If you lose your password, you cannot recover it yourself.
  • MyBudget is free while in testing, and the app may change before it leaves testing.
