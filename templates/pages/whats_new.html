What's new

What has changed in MyBudget recently.

11 November 2026

  • Added a contact form and an About page, so you can reach the person behind MyBudget.
  • You can now delete your account yourself from your profile. Everything in it is removed right away.
  • Added a demo: try MyBudget with example data, no sign-up needed.
  • Rewrote much of the text on the site to be clearer and more consistent. Two menu items were renamed: Compare is now Yearly overview, and Activity is now Change history.

10 November 2026

  • Your currency now shows on every amount, including charts, forms and your change history.
  • Amounts now use one format everywhere, for example 1,800.00 RON.
  • New accounts start with a Cash wallet and common categories, and today's date is filled in when you add a transaction.
  • Added a privacy page that explains what is stored and how it is protected.
  • Made buttons easier to tap on phones, added a clearer "page not found" page and combined the export buttons into one Export menu.
