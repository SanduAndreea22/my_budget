Questions

Is it free?

Yes. MyBudget is free while it is in testing, and no credit card is needed.

What happens after testing?

Pricing hasn't been decided yet. Until then it is free, and you can export your data or delete your account yourself at any time.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. The demo gives you a private account filled with example data. Change anything you like. It is deleted when you log out, when you sign up, or after about a day.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add transactions yourself, so nothing is imported from your bank.

Which currencies can I use?

You choose one currency for your account (RON, EUR, USD or GBP). It is shown on every amount, chart and export, and you can change it in your profile.

Do budgets stop me from spending?

No. They warn you: a bar turns amber near the limit, red when you are over it, and your dashboard shows an alert.

How do recurring transactions work?

Add your salary, rent or a subscription once and pick the day of the month. From then on it is added for you every month.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. MyBudget runs in your phone's browser, so there is nothing to install.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Export your transactions as CSV, Excel or PDF from the Transactions page. If you have filtered the list, the export only includes what you see.

Who makes MyBudget, and how do I get in touch?

MyBudget is made by Andreea Sandu of Andreea Tech, and the source code is public on GitHub. To get in touch, send a message through the contact form.

What if I forget my password?

There is no password reset yet, so keep it somewhere safe. If you get locked out, send a message through the contact form.
