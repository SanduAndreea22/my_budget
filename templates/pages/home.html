Questions

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. "Try the demo" opens a private copy of the app filled with example data: change anything you like. It is deleted when you log out, when you create a real account, or after about a day.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add transactions yourself, which keeps things simple and private: nothing is pulled from your bank.

Is it free?

Yes, MyBudget is free while it is in testing, and no credit card is needed. The app may change before it leaves testing.

Which currencies can I use?

You choose one currency for your account (RON, EUR, USD or GBP). It is shown on every amount, chart and export, and you can change it in your profile.

Do budgets stop me from spending?

No. They warn you: a bar turns amber near the limit, red when you are over it, and your dashboard shows an alert.

How do recurring bills work?

Add your salary or a subscription once, choose the day of the month, and it is logged for you every month.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. MyBudget runs in your phone's browser, so there is nothing to install.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Export any list of transactions, with your filters applied, as CSV, Excel or PDF.

What if I forget my password?

There is no automatic password reset yet while MyBudget is in testing, so keep your password somewhere safe.
